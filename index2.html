BEWARE OF GOVERNMENT SURVEILANCE!

The government is watching you, but not in the way that you think.

Now thankfully it's not like you've got an FBI agent constantly monitoring your computer screen, because let's be honest, they'd all go completely insane. However, your searches are still recorded and can be looked at if proper legal action is taken.

For example, if you were to search for something illegal, the FBI could use that information to build a case against you. This is why it's important to be careful about what you search for online. However I seriously don't think you should be worried, because you shouldn't be doing anything illegal in the first place.
